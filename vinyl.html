👕 Z&D Vinyl Services

Custom vinyl apparel, available on a project-by-project basis.

Z&D Vinyl Services operates under Z&D Airsoft, LLC and provides custom vinyl-printed apparel for select businesses, organizations, teams, events, and community projects.

Vinyl printing is an additional service we offer rather than the primary focus of our business. We are not currently pursuing high-volume apparel production, but we're happy to consider projects where our equipment and capabilities are a good fit.

🧢 What We Offer

  • Custom vinyl-printed T-shirts and apparel
  • Business, organization, team, event, and fundraiser apparel
  • Small and larger orders considered based on the individual project
  • Custom quotes based on garment, design, quantity, and project requirements
  • Embroidery is not currently offered

💬 Have a Project in Mind?

If you're looking for custom vinyl apparel, send us the details. Let us know what you need, approximately how many pieces you're considering, and any information you already have about the design.

We'll review the project and let you know whether it's a good fit for our current capabilities and availability.

🚚 Delivery & Shipping

  • Free local delivery within 25 miles of Findlay, Ohio
  • Nationwide shipping can be arranged using the customer's FedEx or UPS account for direct billing
  • Customer pickup is not currently available

✅ Our Quality Commitment

When we accept a project, we stand behind the work we produce. If you have a concern with print quality, placement, peeling, or another issue with the finished product, contact us so we can review it and work toward an appropriate solution.

For items that arrive damaged, please contact us within 7 days of delivery. Depending on the circumstances, we can arrange a replacement or refund.

For other workmanship concerns, we'll evaluate the item based on its condition, use, and the nature of the issue. Our goal is simple: if we take on your project, we want you to be happy with the work you receive.
