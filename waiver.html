📝 Electronic Participant Waiver

Required before participating in Z&D Airsoft activities.

All participants must have a completed waiver before participating in Z&D Airsoft Airsoft or NERF activities.

For participants under 18 years of age, the waiver must be completed by a parent or legal guardian.

Safety Still Comes First

Completing the waiver is only one part of getting ready to participate. All participants must also follow Z&D Airsoft safety rules, event instructions, and staff direction while using our equipment or participating in our activities.
