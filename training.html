🎯 Safety & Training

Learn the basics. Build safe habits. Spend more time playing.

Safety is part of every Z&D Airsoft experience. These short videos introduce our safety rules, equipment, smart-target system, and games so participants can arrive familiar with how everything works.

Why start with safety?

The same safety rules apply whether you're using Airsoft or NERF equipment. Muzzle awareness, trigger discipline, proper handling, and respect for anything that launches a projectile are habits we reinforce with every participant.

✅ Start Here: The 5 Weapon Safety Rules — Airsoft & NERF

Every Z&D Airsoft experience begins with safety. These five weapon safety rules apply whether you're using one of our Airsoft blasters, a NERF blaster we provide, or your own compatible NERF blaster at an event.

✅ How to Use the Airsoft Blasters

Get familiar with the equipment before your turn. We'll walk you through the basic operation of our Airsoft blasters and how they're used on the range.

✅ Navigating the Smart-Target Game Screens

Learn how to navigate our electronic smart-target system, choose your game, and get ready to play.

✅ Game Showcase: Pick Before You Play

Take a look at some of the games available on our smart-target system and decide which challenges you want to take on when it's your turn.

🎯 Ready for the Range?

Watching these videos ahead of time means you'll already understand the basics when you arrive. Our staff will provide any additional instructions needed at the event before you play.
