ADDAPTCO Charity Event — All NERF Day!

Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026

Join Z&D Airsoft for a special All NERF Day supporting ADDAPTCO!

We'll be bringing our mobile smart-target range for a day of family-friendly NERF fun. Z&D Airsoft will provide the darts, and we'll have NERF blasters available for anyone who needs one.

Have your own NERF blaster? Bring it along! You're welcome to use your own compatible blaster and put your skills to the test on our electronic smart-target system.

Ammo Compatibility: Z&D Airsoft will supply standard-length and Pro-length NERF darts. Personal blasters must be compatible with one of these dart types.

Come challenge the targets, have some fun, and help us support a great local cause!
