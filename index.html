ADDAPTCO Charity Event — All NERF Day!

Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026

Join Z&D Airsoft for a special All NERF Day supporting ADDAPTCO!

We'll be bringing our mobile smart-target range for a day of family-friendly NERF fun. Z&D Airsoft will provide the darts, and we'll have NERF blasters available for anyone who needs one.

Want to bring your own blaster? Absolutely! You're welcome to bring your own compatible NERF blaster and put your skills to the test on our electronic target system.

Ammo Compatibility: We will be supplying standard-length and Pro-length NERF darts only. Please make sure any personal blaster you bring is compatible with one of these dart types.

Come have some fun, challenge the targets, and help us support a great local cause!
