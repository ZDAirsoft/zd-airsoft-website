🎯 Event Pricing & Options

Flexible options because no two events are exactly alike.

Z&D Airsoft brings mobile Airsoft and NERF smart-target entertainment directly to your event. We work with community organizations, businesses, schools, festivals, fundraisers, families, and other event hosts to find an arrangement that makes sense for the event.

🏢 Community & Business Events

Want to provide the Z&D Airsoft experience to your guests without charging them to play? Our standard host-paid event pricing is simple:

$100 per hour for the first 5 hours

$50 per hour for each additional hour

No additional charge for setup or tear down.

This option works well for businesses, community organizations, schools, employee events, festivals, customer appreciation events, and organizations that want to provide the experience as an included activity for their guests.

🎪 Festivals, Fairs & Public Events

Public events don't always fit neatly into an hourly rental model. Depending on the event, Z&D Airsoft can also work with organizers using a revenue-share arrangement, an upfront vendor fee, or another agreed-upon arrangement that allows us to operate on a Pay Per Play basis.

Pay Per Play pricing is determined based on the individual event rather than using a one-size-fits-all price list. Factors such as event length, expected attendance, event structure, and the agreement with the organizer can all affect the best approach.

Tell us about your event and we'll work with you to determine which option makes the most sense.

🎉 Private Parties & Family Events

Birthdays, quinceañeras, anniversaries, graduations, family gatherings, and other private celebrations are different from large public events — and we don't believe every family should have to fit into the same predetermined package.

Tell us what you're planning and what you're comfortable spending. We'll work with you to put together an experience that meets your needs and your budget.

Use the booking request form below to tell us about your event. The more information you provide, the better we can understand what you're looking for and find an option that works for your family.

❤️ Fundraisers & Charitable Events

Community involvement is an important part of Z&D Airsoft. We're happy to talk with schools, nonprofits, businesses, and community organizations about fundraisers, charity events, and other opportunities to support a good cause.

Because every fundraiser is different, contact us with the details and we'll discuss what may work for your organization and event.
