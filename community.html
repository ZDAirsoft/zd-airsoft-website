Supporting the community that supports us.

❤️ Our Commitment

Community service has been part of Z&D Airsoft's mission from the beginning. We're proud to support local schools, charities, and organizations working to make a meaningful difference in the lives of our neighbors.

We make a charitable contribution each quarter, but our commitment isn't dependent solely on how busy the business happens to be. During slower parts of the year, our owners personally contribute to help keep that commitment going.

💛 Our Commitment Continues Year-Round

Z&D Airsoft is committed to supporting our community every quarter. We pledge a portion of our business profits to local charities and causes, and during slower quarters, our owners personally supplement those contributions to help ensure our commitment continues year-round.

During quarters when business profits are limited or nonexistent, our owners may personally supplement — and at times fully fund — these contributions so that our support of local organizations can continue.

🎯 More Than Donations

Supporting our community isn't limited to writing a check. Z&D Airsoft also works with local organizations, festivals, schools, and charitable causes by bringing our mobile smart-target experience directly to community events.

Whether we're helping support a fundraiser, participating in a community celebration, or simply giving families something fun and memorable to do together, we're grateful for every opportunity to be part of the communities we serve.

Have a Community Event or Fundraiser?

If your organization is planning a fundraiser, festival, school event, or community gathering and you think Z&D Airsoft could be a good fit, we'd be happy to hear from you.
